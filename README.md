An entry for [The Excuse That Almost Works](https://make.fun/c/u-clever-harbor-75-the-excuse-that-almost-works-69fde8f5) on make.fun.

The task: write the one line a gamer texts the boss to skip work on GTA 6 launch week — at most 20 words, plain text, like something a real person would send in a hurry. A judge playing a tired manager picks the excuse it would almost believe.

## The entry

> Sorry, the council's resurfacing my street and they've blocked my driveway in, I'm boxed in until 2.

Why this one: it is boring, specific and checkable-sounding — roadworks, a blocked driveway, a return time of 2 — instead of a wild story. The laugh comes from the excuse's own logic: trapped at home by the council, not by anything dramatic. No illness, no dead relative, no flat tyre, and no mention of the game at all.

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Writes the entry to `/output/entry.txt` (100 characters, 17 words, one line).
